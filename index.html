Before we carry on

A few things
worth knowing

Dating apps leave so much unsaid. So here, in my own words, is what matters to me and what I'm hoping to build with someone.

A small confession first: I used AI to help shape this page. I'm not afraid of using it, but every word here comes sincerely from my heart.

01 Seeing the divide

There is a real rift between men and women right now, and I want people in my life who see it and understand it. The noise is loud on every side, and I'm looking for someone who chooses connection over camps.

02 Healing, together

Most of us carry wounds, and relationships are where they surface. I'm looking for a long-term partnership where we can heal together, rather than keep bumping into each other's hurts until things fall apart.

If you haven't been to therapy, or wouldn't be open to it, we're probably not the right fit.

03 Equality, and romance

I'm a deeply affectionate, somewhat old-fashioned romantic, and I value equality. I'm not looking to lean on a man or to make a relationship my whole world, and I have no wish to diminish or harm a man either.

If you're immersed in the manosphere, it likely won't work between us. I find it promotes genuinely harmful views about women. For the record, I see some of the same harm coming from parts of feminism, too.

04 Devoted to my work

I'm wholeheartedly committed to my work. I'm building my own financial stability, and it asks for real care and attention. I'd love a partner who understands and respects that.

05 A life in motion

I travel for work, and I love to travel. If you're hoping for someone who is always close to home, I'm not that person. I'm also quite clear that Canadian winters are not for me.

Ideally, you're comfortable with some distance for a while as we find our way, or you'd enjoy travelling with me.

06 On intimacy and porn

Porn is distorting modern dating. If you're into it and haven't looked at how it affects men and women, and how it clouds our sense of what real relationships and real intimacy are, you're probably not my person.

07 Meeting in understanding

I'm deeply curious and thoughtful, and I've spent a great deal of time with everything written here. I'm hoping to meet someone who already holds his own understanding of these issues, so our conversations can begin somewhere deep rather than at the very start.

I'm not looking to be a teacher or a coach in my relationship. If these subjects are new to you, we likely won't share much common ground.

With all due respect, if any of this doesn't resonate with you, please don't carry on. And if it does, I'd love to hear from you.

Natalie
