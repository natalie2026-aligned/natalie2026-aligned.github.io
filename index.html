Hello, I’m Natalie

If you’ve followed the link from my profile, thank you for being curious enough to click.

01 What I make

I’m an artist and an entrepreneur working in the arts, and I also facilitate healing work through art. I work with abstract forms drawn from the natural world, guided by a quiet sense that everything around us is alive. Helping others find their way through creativity is some of the most meaningful work I know.

02 Where I live

My life moves between Canada and Mexico. I love the ocean light in one and the colour and warmth of the other. I don’t love Canadian winters, and I’ve built a life that lets me leave them. My dog comes along for most of it.

03 Where I belong

Nature is my home ground. I’m not much of a city person, and I see my future among trees and open sky rather than concrete.

04 How I love

I’m affectionate and a little old-fashioned. I love being courted, and I love to court in return. I’m also wholly myself: I’m not looking for someone to lean on or to build my life around. I want two whole people who choose each other, as equals.

05 What draws me

I’m drawn to men who have looked honestly at their own shadow and can take accountability with grace: someone who has done real healing work, whatever form it took, and remains curious about it. I’m continuously doing my own inner work and growth, and I’ll offer you that same wholehearted commitment. I’d love a relationship where we keep growing, and healing, side by side.

06 A life in motion

My work matters deeply to me, and it takes me places. I’d love someone with his own sense of purpose, who can hold a little distance gracefully. It would be lovely if you have some flexibility in your life and are able to travel, too.

A few things, gently and plainly

I care about the growing rift between men and women, and I’m hoping for someone who chooses connection over camps. The manosphere isn’t for me, and I’m equally wary of the harm in parts of feminism.

I’m interested in how our culture, porn included, shapes the way men and women meet in intimacy. I’d love someone who has given it real thought.

And I’m hoping for a partner who already understands these things, so we can meet on equal ground and our conversations can begin somewhere real.

If something here feels familiar, I’d love to hear from you. Tell me what drew you in, and what you’re making of your own life.

— Natalie
